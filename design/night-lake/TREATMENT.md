# Đêm bên hồ — web scene treatment

## Brief and adaptation
Recreate the five supplied night references as an interactive, code-built wedding setting. Use Lemo-Opuscar's deterministic picture function, camera grammar, authored treatment and visual review. The user's reference palette and slow reading stops override Tilt-Shift Miniature's sunny time-lapse grammar. This is a browser experience, not a narrated MP4. Existing daytime and ceremony wardrobe states remain in the original daytime experience. Night characters are miniature silhouettes in the Western clothing visible in the five references.

## Three candidate structures
1. Five disconnected illustration plates: close visual match, but cannot reverse a physical journey.
2. A continuous lakeside promenade: a high opening, a descending route, and a final flower arch. Chosen for spatial continuity and readable stops.
3. Orbit around one ceremony platform: reveals scale but does not follow the route in the reference images.

## Arc and benchmark
Two people move from an elevated garden to a shared threshold at the water's edge. Setup: lantern terrace. Turn: the curving illuminated bridge reveals the arch. Ending: the arch frames the couple.
Benchmarks: the supplied five images for navy/ivory/gold, flowers, moonlit lake and the direction toward the arch; Tilt-Shift Miniature for aerial observation and intimate scale. Do not copy the demo's town, plot, music, actors or timeline.

## Shots / interactive beat sheet
0. High oblique terrace, locked while title is visible.
1. Descend behind the couple, scene on the left and invitation on the right.
2. Wider rising view of the promenade, album on the right.
3. Track along the shore, event card on the left.
4. Approach the flower platform, couple on the right, RSVP on the left.
5. Close view near the arch, wishes on the left.
6. Frontal arch and final thank-you. Echo the opening's framing.
Each move uses quintic easing in the empty scroll gap, no motion during reading. Reduced motion uses still compositions. Autoplay preview uses the same deterministic render(t), 42 seconds, six seconds per stop/transition.

## Sound and type
No automatic sound or narration. Existing browser music stays opt-in. Ivory serif headings and gold labels are readable DOM text. Never embed UI text in scenery. Static Pages forms explicitly do not submit or save data.

## Delivery
Self-contained static assets in docs/, editable source in public/, deploy through GitHub Pages. No runtime CDN dependency for the scene. Procedural seeded vegetation, flowers, lights, mountains and water; no external raster assets in the night scenery.
