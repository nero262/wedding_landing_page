## western

Use case: identity-preserve. Edit target: the supplied transparent 3-by-2 atlas of the same bride and groom porcelain wedding dolls. Create a production-ready CLEAN TRANSPARENT sprite atlas, exactly 3 equal columns and 2 equal rows, six fully separated full-length couples, no overlaps across tile borders, generous padding around each couple. Preserve the SAME faces, skin tone, brown hairstyle/updo, eye shape, relative heights, groom on the LEFT and bride on RIGHT in every cell. Maintain the same softly sculpted porcelain doll aesthetic, natural hands and cloth, gentle realistic wedding styling. Outfit for ALL six cells: Western daytime wedding. Bride ivory flowing wedding dress and delicate light veil, groom deep NAVY suit, white shirt and restrained bow tie, no heavy accessories. Six poses left-to-right top row: (1) stand together, gently hold hands; (2) bride gently rests toward groom shoulder, groom relaxed; (3) stand upright together holding the same small ivory bouquet between them. Bottom row: (4) stand respectfully with a SMALL inclination of the heads, hands calm in front, no deep bow; (5) stand very close, quietly clasp hands low, warm expressions; (6) stand close, gently angle their gaze to their right towards a ceremony, bodies remain nearly front-facing. Replace reference's waving and heart-making poses entirely with the calm poses above. Avoid waving, large gestures, hearts, additional people, extra flowers beyond the original small bouquet, ceremonial ritual actions, text, labels, grid lines, pedestals, cast shadows outside the bodies, background, halos. Portrait tile proportions are 2:3, the overall atlas is square. Consistent head and foot alignment per tile. Preserve identity across all poses. No paper texture or colored pattern in transparent pixels. This is an illustration for the website, not an image of real people.
